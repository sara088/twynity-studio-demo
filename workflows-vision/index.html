Experimental · not for design

Your twyn runs your workflows.

Not a builder. Twynity is the layer above the builder — you tell your twyn to run something and watch it happen. The workflow can be built anywhere; the magic is running it, seeing it, and capturing it.

01The reframe

Three layers — and we only own the top two

Tom's constraint, made concrete: keep the builder invisible, own the orchestration and the experience. This is how we borrow the Tavus “you talk, it's done” feeling without reinventing n8n.

Build

Invisible · external

Power Automate, UiPath, n8n, LangFlow — or our own engine. Users never see a node canvas. We don't reinvent the builder.

Orchestrate

Twynity's core

The twyn decides which workflow, tool, or teammate to invoke — the decision layer above the models. A workflow is just a capability you equip.

Experience

The differentiator

You talk, it runs — live, on screen, with an approval gate and memory of how you like it done. This is the part only Twynity has.
